Self-contained demo: bookings, guest notes and the waitlist live only in your browser and reset when you clear it. It enforces the real API's rules: one-hour slots, party sizes capped by table, no double-booking a confirmed slot, and cancelling frees the slot. The guest booking page shares the same data, so a booking made there appears here straight away.
